Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Purpose

HOLODORI-rclone is a personal-use configuration of rclone for synchronizing locally generated game notice records with Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file scope to create and update files authorized for use by the application.

Data Handling

Files are transferred between the user's local computer and the user's Google Drive account through rclone.

No independent application server is operated to collect or store Google Drive file contents.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally by rclone and are not published on this website.

Access Revocation

The user may revoke Google Drive authorization through their Google Account security settings.
